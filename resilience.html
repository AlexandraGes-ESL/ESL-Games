Reading

The Personality Problem

The staff at Paws & Co. had twelve cats, twelve very different personalities, and a waiting list of families who all wanted "a nice, friendly cat." The trouble was that not every cat agreed on what "nice" meant.

Take Cleo. She was assertive — she knew what she wanted. If you opened a tin of tuna, she appeared within seconds.

Then there was Biscuit — generous and reliable. If a new kitten arrived feeling frightened, Biscuit would always sit beside them.

The newest arrival, a shy tabby named Pixel, had not left her box in three days. The staff were thoughtful. If they had rushed her, she would have become more anxious. Instead, she came out on her own terms.

IF Conditionals

Type Usage Example
Zero Always true facts / habits If you open tuna, she appears.
First Real future possibility If a kitten arrives, Biscuit will sit with them.
Second Imaginary present/future If I were a cat, I would sleep all day.
