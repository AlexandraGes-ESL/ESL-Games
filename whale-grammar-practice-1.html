Warm-up · Theory

The 3 Golden Rules

Compare these two tenses to understand exactly what you want to emphasize.

Simple Continuous State Verbs
1 The Result vs The Activity ▾
have/has+V3 / -ed
SimpleFocus on the result. The action is complete.
"I have painted my room." (The room was green, now it's yellow).
have/has been+V-ing
Cont.Focus on the activity itself. It doesn't matter if it's finished or not.
"I have been painting my room." (My clothes are covered in paint).
2 How much? vs How long? ▾
We use different tenses depending on what question we answer.
SimpleHow much / How many / How many times?
"She has written ten emails this morning."
Cont.How long?
"She has been writing emails for two hours."
3 State Verbs (Never Continuous) ▾
Some verbs are normally not used in the continuous form: know, like, believe, own, understand, belong.
✔I have known her for five years. (Present Perfect Simple)
✘I have been knowing her.
