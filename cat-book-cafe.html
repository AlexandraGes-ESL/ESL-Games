The story

You’ve just rented a tiny attic flat above The Copper Kettle — a tea shop where the kettle whistles by itself and the cat gives suspiciously good advice.

It’s perfect. Almost. The rent is far too high for one person, so you’ve pinned a note to the door:

“Looking for a flatmate. Must be kind. Must like tea. Must not steal my biscuits.”

Six people replied. Six interviews tonight. The candle is lit — and somehow, by its light, you can see what a person is really like.

Marmalade
Tea-shop cat · unpaid advisor

“So. Six people answered your little note. I read all of them, obviously — I read everything.”

“Here’s the trick, and I’ll only say it once: people tell you who they are in the small things. Every single one of them wrote the word kind in their letter.”

“But kind is cheap. Look at what they do at three in the morning. That’s where the truth lives.”

— Marmalade
