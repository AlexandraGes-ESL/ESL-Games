    Grammar: "To Be" 📚

    Present Simple of "to be":
    I am 14. I am a figure skater.
    You are smart. You are into gaming.
    He/She is a pilot. It is exciting.
    I want to be + noun:
    I want to be a pilot.
    I want to be a professional gamer.

    ✍️ Now you try: Complete: "I am ___. I want to be ___."

We are going to play games and eat cake. I'm happy and excited!
