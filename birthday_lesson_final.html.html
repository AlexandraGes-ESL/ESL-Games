Extra Task (Homework or Free Time) 📝

Writing Task: "My Perfect Birthday"

Write 6-8 sentences about your ideal birthday.
Use Present Simple at least 3 times (what people usually do, what you usually do).
Use have/has at least 2 times (e.g., "My party has...").
Use some/any (e.g., "I want some gifts" or "I don't have any regrets").
Past Simple too! What happened yesterday? (at least 2 sentences)

Example: "My perfect birthday is in summer. I usually celebrate with friends. My family has a big cake. Yesterday I got some amazing gifts..."
