Grammar Diagnostic+25 XP each

Grammar time.

Four quick questions — each targets a structure that often costs B1 students points on TOEFL Writing & Speaking.

01
Present Perfect vs Past Simple
She ___ to three conferences this year.
A) went
B) has been
C) was going
02
Gerund vs Infinitive
I enjoy ___ academic articles before bed.
A) to read
B) read
C) reading
03
Articles · Uncountables
Could you give me ___ on my essay?
A) some advice
B) an advice
C) some advices
04
Subject–Verb Agreement
The data ___ that students improve faster with feedback.
A) shows
B) show
C) is showing
